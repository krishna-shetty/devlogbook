About Me

I’m Krishna Shetty, though I go by Kris. I’m a graduate student at the University of Southern California, pursuing a Master’s in Computer Science. A non-specialized degree on paper, but one I’ve largely shaped around game development. I previously completed my Bachelor’s in Technology in Computer Science with Honors in Data Science from K.J. Somaiya College of Engineering, Mumbai.

Coming from a computer science background with a bent toward game development, I’ve been fortunate to build both the foundation of a designer and the habits of a developer. I’m fixated on software design at all levels, from high-level architecture to the low-level of systems programming. I firmly believe software should be written to communicate efficaciously with humans first; machine understanding is a fortunate by-product.

My work spans software design across the stack: from custom memory allocators and job systems, to gameplay ability frameworks with state-driven modifiers, to designer- and developer-facing menu-driven tools that eliminate redundancy and streamline development workflows. Game development is simply the outlet for my programming. Because games are, quite frankly, the best thing ever!

If learning is a hobby, it’s my favorite one. Then there are games, duh! I love music to the point of snobbery. I enjoy listening to people talk about things I know nothing about, personal or professional. I walk a lot, read mostly non-fiction (some fiction), and plan my outfits a day in advance.

I’m building my portfolio as a blog, because I document obsessively. It’s meant to be a dive into my reasoning, because code is excellent at explaining how, but rarely why.

If any of this resonates, feel free to reach out. Teach me about software design, games, or any technology that follows a human-first philosophy.
